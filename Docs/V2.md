### The ones I'd add first

#### 1. Inventory

Probably the biggest missing piece.

Show:

- Current stock
- Low-stock products
- Out-of-stock products
- SKU
- Stock adjustments
- Stock history
- Inventory value
- Restock threshold

You can have alerts like:

> ⚠️ 8 products are running low
> 🔴 3 products are out of stock

Inventory management and low-stock alerts are standard features in mature commerce systems. ([Shopify][2])

---

#### 2. Shipments

Separate **order management** from actual fulfillment.

Order:

`Pending → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered`

Shipment page:

- Tracking number
- Courier
- Shipping fee
- Delivery status
- Expected delivery
- Customer address
- Failed delivery
- RTO

This is particularly useful if Sorrel is intended for Pakistan, where courier/COD operations can become a major part of the business. ([Aghaz][3])

---

#### 3. Returns & Refunds

This deserves its own module.

```text
Return Requests
Approved
Rejected
Received
Refunded
Exchanged
```

Each request could show:

- Order
- Customer
- Product
- Reason
- Images
- Return status
- Refund amount
- Admin notes

Returns and refund workflows are common components of serious e-commerce admin systems. ([Abbacus Technologies][4])

---

#### 4. Coupons & Discounts

Create:

- Percentage discount
- Fixed discount
- Free shipping
- First-order discount
- Product-specific discount
- Category discount
- Minimum order amount
- Usage limit
- Expiry date

Example:

```text
WELCOME10
10% OFF
Minimum: PKR 3,000
Uses: 142 / 500
Expires: Sep 30
```

---

#### 5. Reviews

Admin should be able to moderate reviews.

```text
Reviews
├── Pending
├── Published
├── Flagged
└── Rejected
```

Actions:

- Approve
- Reject
- Delete
- Reply
- Feature review

---

#### 6. Analytics

This could make your **Overview** much more powerful.

Show:

**Revenue**

- Today
- This week
- This month
- This year

**Sales**

- Orders
- Average order value
- Conversion rate
- Units sold

**Products**

- Best sellers
- Worst sellers
- Low stock
- Most viewed

**Customers**

- New customers
- Returning customers
- Customer lifetime value

**Charts**

- Revenue over time
- Orders over time
- Sales by category
- Sales by product
- Customer growth

Modern commerce dashboards commonly surface revenue, order trends, best sellers, customer behavior and product performance. ([MyEcomERP][5])

---

### 7. Abandoned Carts

This is a **very good feature** if you're building a serious store.

Example:

```text
Abandoned Cart #1042

Customer: Ali Khan
Items: 3
Cart Value: PKR 8,450
Abandoned: 2h ago

[View Cart] [Contact Customer]
```

Later you could add:

- Recovery email
- WhatsApp reminder
- Discount offer
- Recovery rate

---

### 8. Customers → make it much deeper

Instead of just a customer table, clicking a customer should give you a full profile:

```text
Ali Khan

Total Spent     PKR 84,500
Orders          17
Average Order   PKR 4,970
Returns         2
Last Order      Sep 4, 2026

ORDER HISTORY
PAYMENTS
RETURNS
REVIEWS
ACTIVITY
ADDRESSES
```

This turns Customers into an actual **CRM-lite system**.

---

### 9. Content / Storefront

If you're controlling the whole store from Ops:

- Homepage banners
- Featured products
- Collections
- Navigation
- Promotional sections
- Pages
- FAQs
- Blog
- SEO metadata

Some modern commerce dashboards even include themes, menus, media libraries and storefront content management. ([Aghaz][3])

---

### 10. Team & Roles

Very useful if more than one person operates Sorrel.

Roles:

```text
Owner
Admin
Manager
Order Manager
Inventory Manager
Support
Marketing
```

Then permissions:

```text
Orders       ✓
Products     ✓
Customers    ✓
Refunds      ✕
Settings     ✕
Analytics    ✓
```

---

### 11. Activity Log

This is especially important for an admin system.

Example:

```text
09:42 AM  Ahmed changed product price
09:38 AM  Basit refunded order #10482
09:31 AM  Sara updated inventory
09:15 AM  Ahmed created coupon SUMMER20
```

You can eventually track:

- Who
- What
- When
- IP/device
- Previous value
- New value

```text
SORREL
└── Ops

    CORE
    ├── Overview
    ├── Orders
    ├── Products
    ├── Categories
    └── Customers

    OPERATIONS
    ├── Inventory
    ├── Shipments
    ├── Returns & Refunds
    └── Abandoned Carts

    GROWTH
    ├── Coupons & Discounts
    ├── Promotions
    ├── Reviews
    └── Storefront

    FINANCE
    ├── Payments
    ├── Transactions
    └── Refunds

    INSIGHTS
    ├── Analytics
    └── Reports

    SYSTEM
    ├── Team & Roles
    ├── Activity Log
    ├── Notifications
    └── Settings
```

**If this is your project, I wouldn't add everything at once.** I'd prioritize:

**Inventory → Shipments → Returns → Coupons → Reviews → Analytics → Activity Log → Settings**

That would take Sorrel from a basic **Products/Orders/Customers CRUD panel** to a much more convincing production-style e-commerce operations dashboard.
